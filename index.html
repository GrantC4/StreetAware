Privacy Policy

PatrolMap ("the app") is developed by Grant Clute ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the app uses and how. In short: the app does not collect, store, or share your personal information or location history.

Information the app does not collect

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. I do not receive, store, or have access to your location, your movements, or any other personal information from the app.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device's location while the app is open to show where you are on the map and to display your current street, estimated address and block, ZIP code, city, speed, and direction of travel.

  • Your location is processed on your device and is not saved as a history.
  • To look up the nearest address, the app sends your current coordinates to Apple's mapping service using Apple's built-in iOS features. These requests are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. I do not receive them.
  • The app stops using location when it is not on screen.
  • You can change or revoke location permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Mile marker data

If you choose to download mile markers, the app requests the statewide list of highway reference posts from the Iowa Department of Transportation's public data service. This request does not include your location. The data is saved on your device so it works offline, and you can delete it in the app's settings.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your settings (such as colors and display options) and any downloaded mile marker data only on your device. This information is not sent to me. Deleting the app removes it.

Copying coordinates

If you choose to copy your coordinates, they are placed on your device's clipboard. The app does not send them anywhere.

Third parties

  • Apple provides maps and address lookups through iOS.
  • Iowa Department of Transportation provides public mile marker data (only when you choose to download it; no location is sent).

The app does not sell or share any information with anyone.

Children

The app is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Support

PatrolMap provides at-a-glance location awareness for public safety and field personnel: a map that follows you, your current street, estimated address and block, ZIP code, city, speed, direction of travel, and highway mile markers — without needing a destination.

Important: Street names, addresses, blocks, ZIP codes, cities, and mile markers are estimates from map data and GPS, and they can be wrong. Always verify a location before relying on it for dispatch, reporting, or emergency response. Set up the app before you start moving, or have a passenger operate it. PatrolMap is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government agency or police department.

Frequently asked questions

The app says "Location access is off."
Tap Open Settings on the warning, or go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services, find the app, and choose While Using the App.

Speed or direction shows "—".
Speed and direction come from GPS and need a clear view of the sky. Direction only appears once you are moving. Wi-Fi-only iPads do not have GPS and may not show speed or direction.

The street name says "Last known street."
Some roads have no nearby addresses. The app shows the last named street (in orange) so you are never left with a blank — treat it as approximate.

How do I get mile markers?
Open Settings → Map → Mile markers and tap Download Iowa mile markers. They are stored on your device and work offline. Markers appear when you zoom in to highway level. Iowa primary routes only for now.

How do I copy my coordinates?
Turn on Settings → Display → Coordinates, then press and hold the coordinates on the map screen and choose Copy coordinates.

The map isn't following me.
Moving the map stops following. Tap the location button in the bottom corner to recenter, or turn on Settings → Camera → Auto-recenter.
